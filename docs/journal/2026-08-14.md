## The story

**Situation.** Before any coding, I had to lock three decisions S2 packaging depends
on: where vectors live, which Bedrock models we call, and which papers we are allowed
to ingest.

**Task.** For each ADR I listed options, wrote honest trade-offs, and made a call I
could defend in review.

**Action.** On the vector store I compared OpenSearch Serverless, pgvector (RDS /
Aurora), and FAISS in Lambda. OpenSearch and pgvector both have always-on cost floors
above the ≤$5/month storage budget we set. FAISS has no idle store cost, so it won
on budget. I also measured the Lambda package: `faiss-cpu` 1.15.0 + `numpy` 2.5.2
unzip to 123 MB (2026-08-12), which leaves enough margin under the 250 MB unzipped
quota that zip deploy works and we do not need a container for S2.

On models I picked Titan Text Embeddings V2 (English corpus, about 5× cheaper than
the Cohere alternative, no quality case for the extra cost), Claude Haiku 4.5 for
generation (cheaper than Sonnet, better at following a refuse path than Nova Micro),
and Amazon Nova Lite as the eval judge so the generator is not grading itself
(self-preference bias).

On the corpus I chose option B: target 40–50 papers, manifest in git, PDFs in
private S3 (`us-east-2`), topic locked to AI/ML UX dark patterns and digital
well-being, with a required ~15–20% unanswerable slice so refusal gets measured.
Peer-reviewed HCI venues first; English; 2015–2026.

On the billing alarm I used the existing whole-account budget instead of a $5 Lucid-only
one, because this account already had spend from other work. I dropped the ceiling from
$25 to $20, attached email plus SNS topic `lucid-billing-alarms`, and published a test
message so the gate was a delivery, not just a config.

**Result.** Three ADRs I can implement from, and S1 is closed (nine days early).
The caveat on 0002 still stands: the decision is accepted, but I cannot invoke
the models until the support case (or the Sep 5 Plan B) lands. S2 does not need
Bedrock.

## What I was wrong about this week

I treated "Accepted" on ADR-0002 as "I can start calling Bedrock." The model
decision can be accepted while the account still has no invoke access. Those are
separate problems.

I also came in thinking the vector-store call would be about search quality. The
thing that actually decided it was the always-on cost floor.
