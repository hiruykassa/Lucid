# Week ending YYYY-MM-DD

## What I worked on

Two or three lines. Enough to reconstruct the week six months from now.

## The story

**Situation.** What was going on. Enough context that a stranger understands the stakes,
no more.

**Task.** What was specifically yours to solve. Be honest about scope — a story about a
small thing you owned completely is stronger than a vague claim on a big thing.

**Action.** What *you* did, step by step, in the first person singular. This is the
longest section. Include the reasoning, including the reasoning that turned out to be
wrong. "I assumed X, spent a day on it, and the eval numbers said otherwise" is the
sentence that makes a story credible.

**Result.** What changed. With the number. And what you'd do differently.

## What I was wrong about this week

If the answer is genuinely nothing, write "nothing" — but check twice, because a week
with no wrong assumptions usually means a week with no hard problems.
